Short guides on getting the most out of one link.

What is a link-in-bio page?

Platforms like Instagram and TikTok only let you place one clickable link in your profile. A link-in-bio page solves this: that single link opens a small page that gathers everything you want to share, such as your store, latest video, WhatsApp, or booking form.

Instead of changing your bio every time you have something new, you update one page and your followers always find the latest links in the same place.

Why one link beats many

A crowded bio confuses visitors, and people who are confused rarely click. One clean page with clearly named buttons guides them to the next step, whether that is buying, booking, or following you.

It also gives you numbers. With visits and clicks per link, you can see what your audience actually cares about and put the best link at the top.

Five tips for a page that gets clicks

Put your most important link first, use short and specific button names, add a clear photo or logo, and pick a theme that matches your brand.

Keep the list short, around five to eight links, and review your statistics every week. Remove what nobody clicks and move up what works.
